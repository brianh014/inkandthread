# Charters

What the player of a live play (`bench/LIVE.md`) is like. A reader charter is a kind of player and what they enjoy, and presses on nothing. A strain charter puts part of the app under strain. Neither is a script: the player follows the story. Under each, list the plays it has had (date, story), and play the least played first. Add one when a finding suggests a strain none covers. Keep them general, never about one story.

## Reader

- **Puzzle-solver.** Loves piecing a mystery together: looks closely, compares what people say, forms a theory and tests it. Wants fair clues and an answer that was there to find.
  - Played: none
- **Slow burn.** Wants a relationship to build: notices small things, talks, takes time, and cares more about who people are than what happens next.
  - Played: none
- **Cosy.** Plays gently and avoids danger: likes places, meals, small kindnesses and people's lives. Wants a story that stays worth reading with no fight in it.
  - Played: none
- **Impulsive.** Wants things to happen: picks the bold thing, speaks before thinking, never waits around, and gets bored fast.
  - Played: none
- **Ambitious.** Wants power, money or standing in this world, and makes plans over days to get it.
  - Played: none
- **Choices only.** Never types a move: picks the Choice that looks most interesting every time, as a player on a phone does.
  - Played: none

## Strain

- **Ignores the hook.** Turns away from whatever the story opens with, leaves the place and the people, and wants something else. Does the world let them, move on without them, and still surprise them?
  - Played: none
- **Liar.** Tells different people different stories, keeps track of who was told what, and waits to see who finds out. Do people know only what they were told?
  - Played: none
- **Crosses people.** Breaks promises, steals, insults, betrays a friend, then needs their help. Do people hold to it, and do able enemies act?
  - Played: none
- **Courts death.** Puts the main character and those around them in mortal danger again and again, and pushes on when hurt. Are Checks asked, does failure cost, can others die, and do wounds last?
  - Played: none
- **Long way round.** Waits, sleeps, travels and lets days pass between visits, and comes back to places and people left earlier. Does the clock keep, does the world move on, and are places and people as they were left? Suits 45 Turns.
  - Played: none
- **Uses every tool.** Plays an ordinary story but Reverts, Rewrites, Edits, tells the Narrator of changes, renames someone, edits the Journal and asks questions, each where a player might. Does everything after agree with the change?
  - Played: none
- **Vague.** Types short, unclear or odd moves: one word, a bare quote, a question to the air, something impossible, a move that only makes sense from three replies back. Is each read as the main character's own, and does the story go on sensibly?
  - Played: none
